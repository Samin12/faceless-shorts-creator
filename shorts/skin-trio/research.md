## Combination evidence boundary

The cited sources explain individual roles. They do not establish superiority or safety for every person of azelaic acid + tretinoin + this specific balm. “Treatment plus comfort” is an educational synthesis, not a claim of proven synergy. Both actives may irritate. A balm does not cancel that risk. Persistent burning, swelling, or severe irritation warrants stopping and contacting a clinician.
