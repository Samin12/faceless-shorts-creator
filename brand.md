# Samin’s video studio

Create clear educational videos with a strong first frame, concrete visual explanations, and a restrained finish. Match the selected project brand; do not force every topic into the same palette.

For the Samin’s Azelaic Acid fictional skincare demo: warm ivory #F4F0E7, forest #213E36, sage #AFC1A0, peach #E2AB8B. Editorial serif headlines, clear sans-serif labels. 1080 × 1920, 30fps. Keep essential text inside 80px horizontal margins and above the bottom 260px UI zone. No fake testimonials, medical endorsements, guaranteed outcomes or unlabeled simulated results.
